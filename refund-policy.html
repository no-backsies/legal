Refund and Cancellation Policy

NoBacksies has no physical goods, so this policy is short. It covers the joining fee, the commitment amount you hold on a challenge, cancelling before you start, and what happens if you dispute an outcome.

The joining fee

A joining fee is charged once per challenge, at the time you start it — ₹49 for a 14-day challenge, ₹99 for 30 days, or ₹149 for 60 days (see our Pricing page). It is non-refundable once the challenge has started, except under the cancellation window described below.

The commitment amount

You choose a commitment amount when starting a challenge, subject to a minimum that depends on the duration you pick: ₹250 for 14 days, ₹500 for 30 days, or ₹1,000 for 60 days. This is not a fee — it is held for the duration of your challenge and is either refunded to you in full on completion, or forfeited and donated on your behalf if the challenge is not completed, as described below.

On completion: full refund

Complete every required check-in for your challenge, within the weekly grace allowance, and your full commitment amount is refunded to the original payment method you used to pay it. Refunds are processed to that method and typically take 5–7 working days to reflect, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Cancelling before you start

You may cancel a challenge for a full refund — the commitment amount and the joining fee — if both of the following are true:

  • It has been less than 24 hours since you paid, and
  • You have not yet made your first check-in.

To cancel within this window, email support@nobacksies.app with your account email and challenge details. Once either condition above is no longer true, the joining fee becomes non-refundable and the challenge runs its normal course — complete it for a full commitment-amount refund, or let it lapse and the commitment amount is forfeited under the rules below.

On failure: forfeited, not refunded

If a challenge is not completed — more missed check-ins than the weekly grace allowance covers — the commitment amount is not refunded. It is retained by Infinite Product and donated, once the dispute window below has closed, to our charity partner ( name and registration details to be added). Donations are batched and paid out on the 5th of each calendar month, from Infinite Product's own bank account; a forfeited amount under ₹500 is pooled with other forfeits paid out in the same monthly batch. Amounts collected and amounts donated are recorded on a public transparency page inside the app.

Disputing an outcome

If you believe a check-in was rejected in error, or a challenge was marked as not completed in error, you may raise a dispute through the app, or by emailing support@nobacksies.app, within 48 hours of the outcome. We do not execute any donation of a forfeited commitment amount until this 48-hour window has passed, specifically so a genuine dispute can still be resolved in your favor. A forfeit is never applied on an automated judgment alone — every disputed outcome is reviewed by a person before the forfeiture stands.

We aim to respond to a raised dispute within 2 business days of receiving it.

Check-in photos

Each daily check-in for an activity that requires one (currently, Gym) involves a photo captured live through the app. We collect and process this photo solely to verify that a genuine, live check-in was made for that day — we do not use it for any other purpose, and it is never published, sold, or shared with any third party. A check-in photo is retained for the duration of the challenge it belongs to, plus a limited period afterward to support a dispute raised under our Refund and Cancellation Policy, after which it is deleted.

Data deletion and retention

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in the app, or by writing to us at the contact information below — see our dedicated Delete Your Account page for the exact steps. Deleting your account removes your name and email address, and you will no longer be able to sign in. If you have an active challenge in progress, it continues to follow its existing money rules (refund on completion, forfeiture to charity on failure) before your account data is anonymized; we may delay deletion until that challenge is resolved or any open dispute is settled.

Challenge history, financial ledger records (commitment amounts, fees, refunds, donations), and check-in photos are retained after account deletion, no longer linked to your name or email — this is required to keep accurate financial and donation records (for example, so a charity donation receipt tied to a forfeited commitment amount stays accurate after the account behind it is deleted). These records are kept indefinitely as accounting history and are never restored to an active, identifiable account.

NoBacksies

Pricing

No subscription. No hidden charges. One joining fee per challenge, plus a commitment amount you choose and get back if you finish. Both vary by how long a challenge you pick.

Pricing by duration

The joining fee and the minimum commitment amount both scale with the length of the challenge. Longer challenges cost more to join and require a higher minimum commitment.

Plan Duration Joining fee Minimum commitment Presets
Kickstart 14 days ₹49 ₹250 ₹250 / ₹500 / ₹1,000
Standard 30 days ₹99 ₹500 ₹500 / ₹1,000 / ₹2,000
Iron Will 60 days ₹149 ₹1,000 ₹1,000 / ₹2,000 / ₹5,000

Iron Will (60 days) unlocks only after you complete a 30-day Standard challenge — it isn't sold upfront, it's earned.

Joining fee

Charged once when the challenge starts, at the rate for the duration you picked (see the table above). This is the only amount Infinite Product ever keeps, whether the challenge is completed or not. It is non-refundable once the challenge has started — see our Refund and Cancellation Policy for the 24-hour window in which it can still be refunded before that.

Commitment amount

You choose an amount to commit for the length of your challenge, starting at the minimum for that duration (see the table above). The app offers preset amounts for each duration and also accepts any custom amount at or above that duration's minimum. This is not a fee — it is held for you and refunded in full if you complete the challenge, or forfeited and donated to our charity partner if you do not.

No subscription, no recurring charges

There is no monthly or annual plan. Every challenge is a single, one-time transaction: the joining fee plus your chosen commitment amount, charged together and shown to you as two separate line items before you pay. Starting a new challenge later is a separate, independent transaction, priced at whatever duration you pick for it.

A worked example

A 30-day Standard challenge with a ₹1,000 commitment amount:

  • You pay today: ₹1,099 (₹99 joining fee + ₹1,000 commitment amount)
  • If you complete it: ₹1,000 refunded to your original payment method
  • If you don't: ₹1,000 forfeited and donated to our charity partner

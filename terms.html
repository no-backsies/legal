3. What NoBacksies is

NoBacksies is a self-commitment and accountability service. A user selects a habit (currently "Gym" or "Wake Up Early"), a duration (14, 30, or 60 days), and how many days per week they must check in (5, 6, or 7). One grace pass — a day you may miss without penalty — is included per week.

To start a challenge, the user pays a joining fee plus a commitment amount of their choosing. Both the joining fee and the minimum commitment amount depend on the duration selected: ₹49 / ₹250 minimum for 14 days, ₹99 / ₹500 minimum for 30 days, and ₹149 / ₹1,000 minimum for 60 days (see our Pricing page for the full table). Both amounts are charged together in a single payment transaction and are shown to the user as two separate line items before payment.

Each day of an active challenge, the user submits a photo check-in from inside the app. If the user completes the challenge, the full commitment amount is refunded to the original payment method; the joining fee is not refunded. If the user does not complete the challenge, the commitment amount is retained by Infinite Product and donated to our charity partner (name and registration details to be added), as described in Section 5 below.

Where a challenge is not completed, the commitment amount is not refunded to the user. Infinite Product donates an amount equal to the user's forfeited commitment amount to its charity partner. A forfeited amount under ₹500 is pooled together with other forfeits paid out in the same monthly batch. Amounts collected and amounts donated are recorded on a public transparency page inside the app.
